-> Exam type: Viva
-> At first, you will be asked to recite from any random ayah from a specific Surah. Specific Surah will be selected by the course teacher.
-> Then, you'll be asked 4-5 questions (it can differ) from grammars. Frequently asked questions are:
    1. Muslimun, Muslimatun table (Heavy and Light version)
    2. Concept of Rafa, Nasb, Jarr
    3. Flexibility of Ism
    4. Concept of Pronouns
    5. Independent and Attached pronouns table
    6. Harf of Nasb, Jar
    7. Idafa
    8. Concept of Numbers: Singular, Dual Numbers and Plurals
    9. Pointers, Pointers table
    10. Mawsoof and Sifa
    11. Mubtada and Khabar
    12. Types of Sentence - Noun and Verb Based

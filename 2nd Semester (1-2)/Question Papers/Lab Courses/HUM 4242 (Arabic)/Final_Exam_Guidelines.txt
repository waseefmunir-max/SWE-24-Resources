-> Exam type: Viva
-> At first, you will be asked to identify grammars of a sentence of Ayat from specified Surah. This includes - Identification of noun and verb based sentences, sentence fragments and does & object of a sentence etc.
-> Then, you'll be asked 4-5 questions (it can differ) from grammars. Frequently asked questions are:
    1. Nasara Table (both past and present)
    2. Muslimun, Muslimatun table (Heavy and Light version)
    3. Flexibility of Ism
    4. Independent and Attached pronouns table
    5. Particle of Light and Lightest
    6. Harf of Nasb, Jar
    7. Idafa
    10. Mawsoof and Sifa
    11. Mubtada and Khabar
    12. Types of Sentence - Noun and Verb Based
   
Note: There will be no recitation from Surahs, as that was part of 1-1, not 1-2. Instead, you'll be given a random sentence or ayah from Surahs and asked grammatical questions.
